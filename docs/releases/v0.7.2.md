The panel measures wide characters correctly, crops each view to the terminal height with scrolling, and wraps the support line instead of cutting it. Idle sessions stay listed while paused, on a `/model` hold or with a pending pin; restored intent appears at once after a brain restart.

Requests the router cannot route, such as compaction, skip parsing, and their replies no longer count against the previous routed decision. Dollar totals appear only when every eligible reply is priced; otherwise the panel says `estimate partial` or `estimate unknown` with the priced count.

Policy reports a configured model missing from the catalog as compatibility unknown, not blocked: whether it shares the request's compatibility group is unknown, so the route is not among those that never run.

`codex-router doctor` reports config, state, versions, services, catalog, classifier and logs, changing nothing. The panel retries a socket that answers as another endpoint with backoff.

This release builds with Go 1.27.3, whose standard library stops trusting known-compromised intermediate certificates (GO-2026-6809, affecting Go 1.27.2). Building from source needs Go 1.27.3 or newer; the published binaries are built with it.

After upgrading the binary in place, restart the home's services; if it moves, rerun setup.
